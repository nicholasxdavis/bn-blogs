Who we are

Blacnova Development is locally owned and rooted in the Mesilla Valley. We are not a call center that redistributes your project to whoever is free this week. When you talk to us, you are talking to the people who will design, ship, and support the work.

Our bias is practical. Pretty mockups that never launch do not help a restaurant on Avenida de Mesilla or a contractor on the East Mesa. We care about pages that load, forms that deliver leads, search that finds you, and systems that stay online. Security is part of that story too. We put Cloudflare and careful engineering in front of client projects because availability and trust are part of the product. More on that in how we secure client projects.

What we build

Most client work falls into a few clear buckets:

  • Web development. Marketing sites, service sites, landing pages, and custom web applications that match the brand and work on phones.
  • SEO foundations. Technical cleanup, page structure, local signals for Las Cruces and Mesilla searches, and content that can actually rank.
  • E-commerce. Stores with clear product paths, payments, and inventory flows that do not fight the owner every morning.
  • Cloud and hosting posture. Modern delivery, often on Cloudflare-backed stacks, so the site is fast and harder to knock over.
  • API and workflow glue. Connecting tools so data stops living in five disconnected spreadsheets.
  • Ongoing support. Updates, fixes, and a simple monthly plan after launch.

BN Tools and products we ship ourselves

Besides client builds, we make and maintain our own products. That matters because it means we live in the same world our clients do: shipping, iterating, supporting users, and fixing what breaks.

  • BN Tools. A suite for everyday business work: links, forms, email helpers, workspace pieces, invoicing, cards, and related utilities.
  • Cards / content systems. Structured content for menus, products, events, and other JSON-powered blocks owners can update without begging a developer for every comma.
  • TIIO and TeleLink. Our own software experiments and tools, from offline media to local phone bridges for builders and agents. They show how we think about product craft outside client retainers.

Client sites often get access to Blacnova Development dashboard and tool pieces so owners can edit content without waiting on a ticket queue for every blog post or menu change.

How a project runs

We keep the process short and readable:

  1. Discovery and strategy. You fill out the project intake with goals, audience, must-have pages, and constraints. We translate that into scope, timeline, and a quote.
  2. Design and development. Wireframes and production code with feedback cycles you can follow. No mystery month where nobody answers.
  3. Launch and growth. Deploy, connect analytics where useful, harden the basics, then keep improving search and conversion after go-live.

Prefer kicking off in person? We can meet locally. That is one of the quiet advantages of hiring in Las Cruces instead of a national shop that only knows Doña Ana County as a pin on a map.

Pricing without the fog

We quote by scope: pages, features, integrations, and SEO needs. You see a number before we build. After launch, ongoing service and support is typically $25 per month. That is meant to cover the boring, necessary work of keeping a site healthy, not a vague retainer that balloons without shipping.

Bigger apps, multi-location SEO, or complex integrations cost more up front because they take more time. What does not change is the rule: scope first, surprise invoices never as a business model.

Why Las Cruces matters to the work

Southern New Mexico businesses compete on place, trust, and word of mouth as much as on ads. A generic template that could belong to any city wastes that advantage. We design and write with local reality in mind: mobile traffic, “near me” search, bilingual households, tourism seasons, campus calendars around NMSU, and the difference between Mesilla Plaza foot traffic and East Mesa service radius.

We also care about keeping more of the digital economy here. Hire local print. Hire local builders when you can. Use free counseling at SBDC or SCORE when you are stuck on the business plan side. We collected a practical shortlist in our Las Cruces business resources guide.

Who we are a good fit for

We work well with:

  • Restaurants, shops, and hospitality around Las Cruces and Mesilla
  • Trades, clinics, and professional services that need leads and clear service pages
  • Owners replacing a DIY site that looks fine but never shows up in search
  • Teams that need a custom tool, booking flow, or light app, not just a brochure
  • People who want a scoped quote and a human on the other end of the email

We are a weaker fit if you want a giant enterprise committee process, or if you only want the cheapest possible template with no care for performance, ownership, or security. We would rather say no than ship something we would not put our name on.

FAQ

Where is Blacnova Development based?

Las Cruces, New Mexico. We serve Mesilla, the Mesilla Valley, Southern New Mexico, and remote clients when the project fit is clear.

Do you only build websites?

No. Websites are a big part of the work. We also build web apps, e-commerce, integrations, SEO foundations, and our own BN Tools suite.

How do I start?

Go to blacnova.net, tell us what you need, and we will reply with next steps and a scoped quote. You can also browse services and portfolio pieces on the main site first.

Can you take over a site someone else built?

Often yes. Rebuilds, redesigns, SEO cleanup, Cloudflare hardening, and support takeovers are common. We will be honest if the existing stack is too fragile and a fresh build is cheaper long term.

Do you help with branding and AI-generated logos?

We help clients avoid brand choices that create legal and trust problems later. If you are tempted by a free AI logo generator as your permanent mark, read the real cost of AI logos and brand media before you print a thousand cards.
